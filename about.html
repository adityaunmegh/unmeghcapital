Who We Are

UNMEGH CAPITAL is an independent financial advisory and corporate finance firm established with a vision to support businesses, entrepreneurs, promoters, investors and institutions in making informed financial and strategic decisions.

We aim to provide comprehensive financial advisory solutions across the capital structure, combining financial expertise, analytical capabilities, strategic thinking and a relationship-driven approach.

Our focus is to help businesses identify opportunities, optimise their capital structure, access appropriate sources of funding, execute strategic transactions and create sustainable long-term value.

We believe that every business has a unique financial journey. Accordingly, we endeavour to develop solutions based on the client's business objectives, financial position, growth ambitions and risk considerations.

Our Business Philosophy

We believe that finance is not merely about raising capital or completing transactions. It is about understanding businesses, evaluating opportunities, managing financial risks and making decisions that contribute to sustainable growth.

Our philosophy is to understand the complete business before recommending a financial solution.
